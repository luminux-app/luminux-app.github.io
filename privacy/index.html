Luminux Privacy Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

Luminux is a photo gallery for the photos and videos on your iPhone. Your photos never leave your iPhone. The app has no account. What leaves the device is the place lookups you can turn off, and what an ad needs, only when you choose to watch one.

Your photos and videos

  • Your library stays on your iPhone. Luminux shows, organises, favourites, edits and deletes the photos and videos in your library, all on the device. Nothing is uploaded. If you give Luminux access to only some photos, it sees only those.
  • Edits are saved to your library. Rotating and cropping (with Pro) save a new version through Photos, which you can revert there.
  • The journal stays on your iPhone. Luminux groups your photos into moments, days and trips on the device. Notes and titles you add are stored inside the app and are removed when you delete it.
  • The home screen widget shows photos copied from your library into storage shared only with the widget, on your iPhone.

Place names in the journal

To title trips like "a day in goa", Luminux sends the locations of photos taken away from home to Apple Maps and gets back place names. Only the locations are sent, never the photos, and the names are kept on your iPhone. Apple handles these requests under Apple's privacy policy. You can turn this off in Settings → "place names".

Ads

Slideshow, editing and rotating are part of Luminux Pro. Without Pro, you can use one of them once by watching a short ad, or unlock Pro for good with a one-time purchase. Ads only appear when you choose to watch one.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Google's Mobile Ads SDK starts only when you first tap "watch an ad", so if you never do, or you have Luminux Pro, it never runs. Luminux always asks for non-personalised ads and never asks to track you, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is never shared.

When an ad loads, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • a device identifier (not the advertising identifier)
  • your approximate location, estimated from your IP address
  • information about the ads you see and how you interact with them
  • performance and crash data from the ad SDK

Google uses this to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services, as described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent in the EU, UK and similar regions. Where the law requires it, Google's consent form asks for your choices before any ad loads. You can change them later in Luminux under Settings → ad privacy choices.
  • No ads at all. With the one-time Luminux Pro purchase, every feature works without an ad.

Purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether Luminux Pro has been bought, so it can be restored on your other devices.

Children

Luminux isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
